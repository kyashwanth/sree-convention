About the venue

A convention centre Kadapa families can call their own

Sree Convention is a function hall and convention centre in Yerramukkapalli, Kadapa. The hall is bright, air-conditioned and laid out with a raised stage, a red-carpet aisle and rows of neatly covered seating – ready to be dressed for your occasion.

In front of the building, a wide tiled courtyard gives your guests room to arrive, gather and take photographs before they step inside.
